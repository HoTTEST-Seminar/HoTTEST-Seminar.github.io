Title: Is it time for a new proof assistant?
YouTube: https://youtu.be/7oBkEbKJvnE
Slides: Sterling-2025-09-25-HoTTEST.pdf
Abstract: It could be time to build a <em>non-experimental</em> proof assistant for homotopy type theory and univalent foundations. I’ll give my thoughts on what that would entail, and where we are able to contribute in the era of <em>Pax Leanica</em>, focusing on algebraic hierarchies, carefully designed user experience, and a proposed return to foundational orthodoxy.
